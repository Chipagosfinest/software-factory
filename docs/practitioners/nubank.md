# Nubank

*Refreshed 2026-11-02*

## What they do

Nubank's engineering post describes using Cognition's Devin on a migration inside a codebase of over 6 million lines. The vendor case study is cited from the [Cognition Devin](./cognition-devin.md) profile. This profile uses Nubank's page.

## What they do well

The post says Devin completed a project that had been estimated at over 18 months with a thousand engineers, and that the work finished in weeks, with a 12-fold increase in efficiency and significant cost savings. It does not state a 20x cost figure or a count of 100,000 data classes. Those figures are not copied here.
